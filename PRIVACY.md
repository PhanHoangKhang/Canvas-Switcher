# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 27 November 2026**

Canvas Switcher provides quick access to Canvas course links and details through Chrome's side panel. This policy explains what information the extension accesses and how it is handled.

## Information Used

Canvas Switcher uses your existing Canvas sign-in session to retrieve and display:

- Course names and codes
- Announcement titles, messages and dates
- Assignment names, descriptions, due dates and possible points
- Module names, item details and links

The extension does not ask for your Canvas password. It uses your existing authenticated Canvas session to access course information.

## Where the Information Goes

Canvas Switcher retrieves information directly from supported Canvas sites through your browser.

Course information is processed within the extension to display your courses and provide navigation features. It is not transmitted to the developer or shared with third parties.

The information is held in memory while the extension is in use and is not stored for later use.

## Permissions

- **Side Panel:** Displays Canvas Switcher alongside your browser tab.
- **Tabs:** Finds open Canvas tabs and opens the Canvas pages you choose.
- **Instructure site access:** Allows the extension to interact with supported Canvas sites hosted on `*.instructure.com`.

Canvas Switcher uses the information it accesses solely to provide its course overview and navigation features.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Canvas Switcher follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Contact
